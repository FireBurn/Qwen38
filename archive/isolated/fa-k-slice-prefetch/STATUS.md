# K slice fetch ahead

Private bounds-derived shader. Fetches the next 16-column K slice before staging the current slice to LDS and running its QK WMMA. Same two workgroup barriers per slice, same arithmetic and bounds helper, no K/V layout change. Fixed 256 threads, Bc64, HSK256, Q8 K. Inspired by the raw-quant fetch-ahead pipeline in stew675/llama-cpp-rdna-boosts PR195; this smaller slice pipeline does not copy its full-tile register buffering.
