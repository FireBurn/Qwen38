# Research leads checked on 2026-10-26

## RDNA4 issue width

[AMD RDNA4 ISA](https://www.amd.com/content/dam/amd/en/documents/radeon-tech-docs/instruction-set-architectures/rdna4-instruction-set-architecture.pdf), section7.8, restricts VOPD dual issue to wave32 and independent VALU operations. Current qualified mega is wave64, so changing LLVM flags cannot directly enable VOPD for it. A wave32 port would require revisiting wave64 reductions, scheduling and numerical order. This is a structural opportunity, not a verified gain. Avoid assuming WMMA, packed dot or occupancy improvements automatically improve a memory-bound single-query decoder.

## AOTriton FlyDSL attention

[ROCm AOTriton0.14.2b release](https://github.com/ROCm/aotriton/releases/tag/0.14.2b) ships a FlyDSL SDPA backend for gfx1201 and gfx950 and reports substantial attention gains over0.13b on its tested configurations. It also documents precision fixes involving placement of scaling factors. Candidate: inspect gfx1201 layouts, staging and scale placement for transferable ideas. These are not our Vulkan Q8-KV, recurrent-GDN, C1/C2 MTP shapes, and reported library gains do not predict mega gains. No code imported or package installed.

## Narrow KV tiles: useful scope exclusion

[vLLM issue58060](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/58060) reports measured R9700 benefits from a narrower KV tile for embedding/reranking attention. The report explicitly says generation prefill/decode use different paths and were unaffected. Do not treat its headline gains as applicable to this model. Tile sizing remains a hypothesis to test against our actual FA shapes and resource cliff, while preserving integrated FA.

## LLVM versus ACO

[LLVM issue219248](https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/issues/219248) reports packed-integer codegen differences on gfx1151 and gfx1100, with the expected dot instructions present on both backends. It uses HIP wave32 versus Vulkan wave64, and is an issue report rather than an accepted fix or R9700 result. It motivates comparing emitted waits/address generation/occupancy with operationally matched wave sizes. It does not justify replacing our qualified ACO path with AMD LLVM. No new patch from this report has been applied.
