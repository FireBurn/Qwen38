Pinned private common/speculative.cpp already implements draft-mtp for qwen35. Current mega shader has NUM_COLS=1 and current captured operators/qualification cover single-token decode. Those facts do not establish an optimized multi-token verification path: dispatch eligibility, native fallback, causalFA, GDN state sequencing and exact rollback need explicit audit. Merely increasing NUM_COLS is insufficient across the graph. No MTP inference launched, model/server configuration changed or speed claim.

Primary research: https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.17192 proposes speculative verification to amortize target inference while preserving its distribution under the algorithm's assumptions. This can address repeated weight reads rather than only unpacking. Our implementation's finite-precision and recurrent-state behavior must be checked independently; paper speedups do not transfer to R9700.

Relevant upstream PR https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/26827 is OPEN/unmerged at inspection (metadata archived). It proposes serializing MTP multi-ubatches sharing mutable KV; its reported CUDA/meta issue does not establish a Vulkan bug here. Private context source lacks its exact mtp_multi_ubatch marker, which is not by itself proof of missing equivalent serialization. No patch applied.
