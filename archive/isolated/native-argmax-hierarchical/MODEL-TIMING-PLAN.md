# Hierarchical argmax fixed72 model discovery

Prerequisites: stock/wide/hardened-hier 38+136+138+72+146 captured raw target rows per mode, identical tokens and speculative counters, positive hierarchical dispatch in each candidate audit, and no new GPU errors. The model workload is the preserved Q3 LynnStyle MTP1/greedy/q8 KV/FA/context4096 baseline with native allocation, queue and submission defaults. Use the private driver with one-dword copy and transfer queue disabled in every child; this differs from the historical copy2 result, so measure stock and wide controls afresh. Use the same hardened host for wide and hier, the original stock core/common and synchronized timing runner. Audit logging and profiling off during timing. Compare full configuration rather than infer gains from the operator result.

Fixed 72 fresh 512-request-token processes in 12 symmetric six-arm blocks, six permutations twice, each followed by its reverse. Arms: stock CPU sampler, wide shader plus backend temperature-only sampler, hierarchical shader plus the same sampler. Primary contrasts are hierarchical versus stock and hierarchical versus wide, both prespecified. Pair-specific load matching uses mean CPU busy within 5 percentage points, build process count within 2, runnable threads within max(4,15% of pair mean), and valid required telemetry. Natural clock and power are observed, not controlled. Calculate geometric steady-token-rate ratios and descriptive t intervals across blocks; report all and matched subsets, with at least four matched blocks. Require identical 513 tokens and draft counters, unchanged pinned assets/runtime and no new target-GPU errors. Stop immediately on error, timeout or mismatch. No retries, extensions, pooling or CPU/GPU clock changes; background builds are allowed and measured.

Only if both primary matched lower bounds are positive with at least four matched blocks, proceed to an independently predeclared fresh-process confirmation including stock and wide controls. Discovery alone does not establish a confirmed improvement; model equivalence is limited to audited cases. No claim about the persistent mega kernel, arbitrary samplers, contexts or drivers.
